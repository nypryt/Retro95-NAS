Win95 NAS 1.0
=============

A Windows 95-styled network drive that lives on your own Mac.

Install
-------
1. Drag "Win95 NAS.app" into the Applications folder.
2. Open it. The first run copies the app to
   ~/Library/Application Support/Win95NAS and asks where your files
   should be stored.
3. The desktop opens at http://localhost:4040.

Requirements
------------
* macOS 10.13 or newer
* Python 3.11 or newer (python.org build recommended)

The app stores its settings in ~/Library/Application Support/Win95NAS/data
and your files in the folder you choose during setup. Nothing leaves the Mac.
